Source: Dean of Student Affairs (DoSA), Gymkhana IIT Bombay - Housing and Dining Overview

HOSTELS AT IIT BOMBAY:
IIT Bombay has 17 hostels catering to student accommodation. Hostel Nos. 1 to 9, 12, 13, 14, and 15 (A and B wing) are for boys, while Hostel Nos. 10, 10A, 11, and 15 (C wing) are for girls.

COMMON FACILITIES IN EVERY HOSTEL:
- Washing and ironing facilities
- Computer room
- TV / common room
- Sports grounds
- Indoor games: Table Tennis, Carrom Board, Chess, etc.
- Access to most facilities is against the student's I-Card
- Internet connectivity in each room
- Several hostels have in-house shops for daily needs, stationery, xerox, and printing facilities

MESS / DINING:
Each hostel has its own mess, serving food four times a day (breakfast, lunch, snacks, dinner). Several additional food outlets are also spread around campus for students who wish to eat outside their hostel mess.

ROOM RETENTION DURING VACATIONS:
Students currently residing in hostels must follow room retention rules during vacations:
1. Students must submit a room retention form, duly signed by the respective authority, by the assigned date — 25th April for summer vacation, and an announced date for winter vacation.
2. If retention is not approved, the room must be vacated by the assigned date and keys handed over to the Hall Manager. Non-compliance incurs a fine of Rs.500/day.

FURTHER INFORMATION:
Details about individual hostels (specific amenities, hostel-level facilities, and hostel councils) are available via each hostel's own webpage on the Gymkhana portal. Hostel-specific queries should be directed to the respective hostel's General Secretary or Warden, while institute-wide hostel policy queries go to the Hostel Coordinating Unit (HCU) or Dean of Student Affairs (Dean SA).
