Source: Gymkhana IIT Bombay - Hostel 10 Rules
Note: These are illustrative hostel-level rules. Individual hostels at IIT Bombay each publish their own rules; Hostel 10's rules are used here as a representative example of the categories and fine structure that hostel councils typically enforce.

1. DOGS/CATS: Petting or feeding dogs/cats inside hostel premises, or enabling their entry, is not allowed.
   Fine: 1st complaint Rs.1,000/-; 2nd complaint Rs.2,000/-; more than 2 violations may result in revocation of hostel facilities.

2. SMOKING/DRINKING: Smoking and drinking are strictly prohibited in the hostel.
   Fine: 1st complaint Rs.1,000/-; 2nd complaint Rs.2,000/-; more than 2 violations may result in revocation of hostel facilities.

3. DANCE ROOM/GYM ROOM KEY: The key must be returned immediately after use. If not returned and the room is found locked:
   Fine: 1st complaint Rs.500/-; 2nd complaint Rs.1,000/-; 3rd complaint Rs.2,000/- (doubles with each subsequent complaint).

4. WASHROOM USAGE: Hostels designate specific washrooms by gender; residents are expected to accompany friends/guests to washrooms as a safety measure.
   Fine: 1st complaint Rs.500/-; 2nd complaint Rs.1,000/-; more than 2 violations may result in revocation of hostel facilities.

5. GUEST ENTRY: Guest entry is generally allowed from 7:30 AM to 10:00 PM; guests must exit before 10:00 PM. Guests must deposit their ID card with hostel security and sign the visitor book. No guest of the opposite sex may stay overnight without prior permission from the Warden or Dean of Student Affairs (Dean SA).
   Fine for late exit: 1st complaint Rs.500/- per 10 minutes; 2nd complaint Rs.1,000/- per 10 minutes; more than 2 violations may result in revocation of hostel facilities. Leaving an ID card at the security desk also incurs a Rs.500/- fine.

6. ILLEGAL GUEST STAY: Keeping an unbooked guest overnight in a room is prohibited.
   Fine: 1st complaint Rs.10,000/- per guest; 2nd complaint Rs.20,000/- per guest; more than 2 violations may result in revocation of hostel facilities.
   Note: If a resident does not open the room during a security check despite repeated knocking, security may treat it as an emergency and break the door open. Residents are expected to cooperate during security checks.

7. WASHING MACHINE USAGE: Undergarments, shoes, socks, and floor mats may not be washed in shared washing machines/dryers.
   Fine: 1st complaint Rs.1,000/- per item; 2nd complaint Rs.2,000/- per item; more than 2 violations may result in revocation of hostel facilities.

8. PLANTS: Plants are not allowed in hostel rooms.
   Fine: 1st complaint Rs.500/-; 2nd complaint Rs.1,000/-; more than 2 violations may result in revocation of hostel facilities.

9. MESS UTENSILS: Taking mess utensils outside the mess area is not allowed.
   Fine: 1st complaint Rs.500/- per utensil; 2nd complaint Rs.1,000/- per utensil; more than 2 violations may result in revocation of mess facilities.

10. EMERGENCY/FIRE EXIT DOORS: These may not be used except in genuine emergencies.
    Fine: 1st complaint Rs.2,000/-; 2nd complaint Rs.4,000/-; more than 2 violations may result in revocation of hostel facilities. Guests found using emergency exits without proper entry incur a Rs.10,000/- fine and referral to Dean SA.

11. BEHAVIOR: Misbehavior toward PHO (Public Health Office) workers, mess workers, office staff, security, or council members is not tolerated and is forwarded to the disciplinary committee.

12. GENERAL: Complaints and maintenance issues should be reported through the official support portal (support.iitb.ac.in) or the relevant hostel council email (e.g., maintenance or mess committee addresses published by each hostel).

DISAGREEMENT / APPEALS: For any disagreement about a fine or rule application, residents are directed to contact the Dean of Student Affairs (Dean SA).
