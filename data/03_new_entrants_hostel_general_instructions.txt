Source: IIT Bombay (iitb.ac.in) - New Entrants PG/PhD Hostel General Instructions

HOSTEL ACCOMMODATION:
For students who are eligible for on-campus hostel accommodation, hostels are pre-allotted and communicated to students in advance. There are four hostels for male students (Hostel #17, Hostel #18, TANSA, Hostel #4) and three hostels for female students (Hostel #15, Hostel #10, Hostel #11).

New students report to their allotted hostel during specified reporting dates in office hours (10:00 am to 6:00 pm) and complete hostel occupation formalities. Temporary accommodation is provided if a student arrives outside office hours.

GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS FOR NEW ENTRANTS:
- Bring 5 passport-size photographs.
- Help desks are set up in each hostel where students submit a physical hard copy of the pre-filled hostel registration form and other necessary forms. After submission, students receive their hostel room keys.
- Help desks are also set up at the Main Gate of IIT Bombay to guide new students.

ROOM ALLOTMENT NOTE:
Rooms are double-occupancy rooms and students will have a room partner. Hostel authorities reserve the right to reshuffle rooms after the initial allotment to ensure consistent, uniform application of the double-occupancy policy during the student's first year.

ESSENTIALS AND MATTRESS STALLS:
Mattress stalls are available on campus where students can purchase essential items (mattress, pillow, pillow cover, bedsheets, bucket, mug, dustbins, etc.) at subsidized rates. These stalls are located at Hostel 17, Tansa, Hostel 15, and Hostel 10. Vendor contact details are displayed at various campus locations.

MESS ENROLLMENT:
Each hostel has its own mess. To enroll, students must contact the Mess Manager of their allotted hostel after completing hostel occupation formalities. Students must pay the Semester Mess Advance (SMA) and bring proof of payment to enroll in the mess facility.

SUPPORT:
The Institute Hostel Affairs Council and individual Hostel Councils provide assistance with hostel-related matters.

CONTACT ROLES (Hostel Affairs, illustrative structure — contact persons rotate each year):
- General Secretary Hostel Affairs (Institute-level)
- Institute Secretary Hostel Affairs (PG)
- Institute Secretary Hostel Affairs (UG) — multiple secretaries
- General Secretary for each individual hostel (e.g., Hostel 17, Hostel 18, Tansa & 4, Hostel 15, Hostel 11, Hostel 10)
- Warden Nominee for each hostel

For current-year contact names and phone numbers, students should check official hostel affairs communications sent at the start of the academic year, as these roles are re-elected annually.
