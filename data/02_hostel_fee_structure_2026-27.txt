=== NOTES APPLICABLE TO ALL FEE TABLES ===
1. Hostel Fees components are applicable only to students who have opted for hostel facilities. Charges will be NIL for those who have NOT opted for hostel facilities.
2. Academic fee payment is mandatory while paying the Hostel Fees.
3. Hostel Fees must be paid by 15.07.2026. After 15.07.2026, a fine of Rs.100/- per day w.e.f. 16.07.2026 will be charged till the date of payment. Request for a fine waiver will not be entertained.
4. Every UG student staying in the hostel has to pay a Per Semester Mess Advance (SMA). SMA can be paid in full or in two equal installments; the first installment is due by 15.07.2026 and the second by 30.09.2026. Late SMA payment is fined at Rs.100/- per day after the due date.
5. Married Ph.D students staying in quarters (MRSB / Tulsi / QIP, etc.) pay a License Fee as applicable under Estate Office rules.
6. Students paying fees via Bank Loan or Sponsoring Agency (Private/Government) must request the fee amount be transferred directly to the IIT Bombay Bank Account at least 10 days prior to the fee payment deadline (i.e., by 15.07.2026), since reconciliation takes 5-7 working days and Autumn Semester 2026-27 registration is linked to hostel fee payment.
7. Students availing a bank loan or scholarship who could not receive the amount in time should intimate HCU on/before 15.07.2026 and submit documentary proof of the loan/scholarship application submitted on/before 15.07.2026.
8. Failing to pay hostel fees within the prescribed date, or not intimating HCU about an applied loan/scholarship by 15.07.2026, means it will be presumed the student is unwilling to stay in the hostel, and the occupied hostel room will be vacated as per rules.
9. Students who intimate HCU about a loan/scholarship after 15.07.2026 will still be charged a fine of Rs.100/- per day w.e.f. 16.07.2026 till the date of payment of Hostel Fees.
10. If a registered student wants to stay in a hostel only for a short duration in a semester (up to 30 days from the beginning of any semester), the student should pay a Hostel Fee of Rs.3,500/-. For a stay of more than 30 days, full Hostel Fee will be charged. (For example, a Ph.D student staying more than 30 days post thesis submission till the Ph.D viva-voce must pay the full hostel fee; such students may contact HCU.)
11. Pro-rata based hostel fee refund is not applicable.
12. IIT Bombay reserves the right to revise the hostel fee structure in subsequent semesters, as per the recommendation of the competent authority.
13. For any query related to hostel fees / hostel room allotment / refund of hostel fees etc., students are advised to write to the Assistant Registrar HCU (arhcu@iitb.ac.in) with a copy to the HCU office (hcu.office@iitb.ac.in).
