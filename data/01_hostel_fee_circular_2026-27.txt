Source: IIT Bombay Hostel Coordinating Unit (HCU)
Document: Schedule for Payment of Hostel Fees by On-Roll UG, PG & Ph.D Students for Autumn Semester 2026-27
Reference No: HCU/Hostel Fees/UG-PG/Autumn/2026-27
Date: 30.04.2026

All on-roll Undergraduate, Postgraduate and Ph.D students are required to pay their Hostel Fees for Autumn 2026-27 as per the following schedule.

PAYMENT SCHEDULE:
- Payment of Hostel Fees for Autumn Semester 2026-27: 01.05.2026 to 15.07.2026
- Payment of 1st installment of Semester Mess Advance (SMA) for Autumn Semester 2026-27: 01.05.2026 to 15.07.2026
- Payment of 2nd installment of SMA for Autumn Semester 2026-27: On/Before 30.09.2026
- Payment of Hostel Fees with fine of Rs.100/- per day: After 15.07.2026, a fine of Rs.100/- per day will be charged w.e.f. 16.07.2026 till the date of payment of Hostel Fees.
- Payment of 1st installment of SMA with fine of Rs.100/- per day: After 15.07.2026, a fine of Rs.100/- per day will be charged w.e.f. 16.07.2026 till the date of payment of SMA.
- Payment of 2nd installment of SMA with fine of Rs.100/- per day: After 30.09.2026, a fine of Rs.100/- per day will be charged w.e.f. 01.10.2026 till the date of payment of SMA.

NOTE: Request for fine waiver will not be entertained.

PAYMENT PROCESS NOTES:
- All students (including students paying through a Bank Loan/Sponsoring Agency) need to pay fees using the "Online Portal" (https://portal.iitb.ac.in/asc).
- Instructions for online Hostel Fee payment are on the ASC home page (https://portal.iitb.ac.in/asc). Students paying fee through portal (online mode) must ensure that their transaction is completed in all respects.
- Bank Loan / Sponsorship / NEFT: The official Fee Demand for such students, to pay the fees directly to IIT Bombay Bank Account, will have to be generated on ASC Portal only (Bank Loan / NEFT fee payment tab at https://portal.iitb.ac.in/asc).
  a) A demand will be valid for 10 days only, before which the money has to be transferred. In case of delays expected from the agency transferring the fees, a fresh demand has to be generated.
  b) Once the money has been transferred, the student must enter the UTR number and other details on the ASC portal.
  c) Initiate the payment process with the agency at least 15 days prior to the fees deadline as it takes a few days for reconciliation from the bank. Semester registration is linked to fee payment.
  d) Students must not intimate IIT Bombay by email, as done during July 2025 and earlier. Only online fees demand and UTR entry will be entertained henceforth.

The fee receipt will be generated after reconciliation, which may take 5-7 working days. Students need to collect the fee receipt in-person from the Cash Section, IIT Bombay for their record purpose.
